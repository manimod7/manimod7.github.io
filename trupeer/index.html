01 · Problem

What's wrong today

Trupeer's promise is that one screen recording becomes a polished video, a step-by-step doc and 20+ translations. The people who buy it (enablement, customer success, support and product marketing teams) document software that changes every sprint.

That is where the value leaks. Every release moves a button, renames a field or adds a step. The guide still plays, so nothing looks broken to the team that owns it. The person who notices is a customer following along, or now, an AI agent answering from Trupeer's knowledge base.

When the creator does notice, the tools ask them to work out by themselves what changed, then pick between editing text, swapping a recording through merge, or re-recording the whole thing. Translations and shared pages each need their own pass.

Problem statement. Teams can't tell which guide steps no longer match their live product, and fixing one step costs close to what a new guide costs. So libraries drift out of date, trust drops, and renewals hinge on a library nobody has time to maintain.
The stale-guide loop
Day 0

Customer ships a release. "Invite user" becomes "Add member".

Day 0 to 30

12 guides still show the old button. No one at the customer knows which 12.

Day 9

Ask Trupeer and MCP answer with the old flow. Support tickets rise.

Day 31

Someone files a complaint. The creator re-records whole videos and re-runs translations, burning credits.

Illustrative scenario, not customer data.

04 · Users and goals

Who this is for

Primary

Enablement or CS lead

Owns 50 to 500 guides. Wants to know on release day what to touch, and to fix it in one sitting.

Secondary

Product marketer

Ships the release. Wants the "what's new" and the old guides updated together, in every language.

Buyer

Workspace admin

Renews the contract. Wants proof the library is current, and that the AI agents aren't sharing old answers.

Goals
  • Surface stale steps within a day of a product change.
  • Make most fixes possible without a full re-record.
  • One approval updates video, doc, translations and Ask Trupeer.
  • Give admins a library freshness score they can show their team.
Non-goals for v1
  • Fixing guides with no human approval.
  • Crawling customers' apps behind login on our own.
  • Rebuilding the editor or timeline.
  • Guides recorded on desktop apps outside the browser.

06 · How detection works

Use signals Trupeer already has

No new crawler in v1. Each signal is cheap, and they get stronger together.

Signal 1 · v1

Re-check on the creator's next visit

The Chrome extension already sees the page at record time. When the creator next visits the same URL, compare the elements each step clicked: label, role and position. A renamed or missing target flags that step.

Signal 2 · v1

Viewers say "this looks different"

A one-tap flag on each step of shared pages and the knowledge base, plus a drop-off spike at one timestamp. Both point at a step, not a whole video.

Signal 3 · v1

Release trigger

Paste release notes or connect Linear or Jira. Match changed feature names against guide scripts and on-screen text, so likely-stale steps get flagged before anyone visits.

Later

Scheduled checks

For Enterprise: a headless re-check of public pages, or of staging with a service login the admin grants. Only after v1 shows flags are accurate.

08 · Success metrics

How we'll know it worked

North star

Share of knowledge views, by people and agents, served from steps verified in the last 30 days.

Input

Days from change to fix

Median, per workspace. Expect weeks today; target under 3 days.

Input

Fixes without a full re-record

Share of resolved flags that used text update or one-step re-record.

Input

Flag precision

Flags acted on ÷ flags raised. Gates the move to scheduled checks.

Guardrail

Dismissed flags under 20%

Noise kills trust faster than misses do.

Guardrail

Credits per fix

Must drop vs today's re-record, or the feature fails its own promise.

Renewal and expansion

Score on every admin dashboard; agent freshness and scheduled checks on Enterprise.

09 · Plan

Four weekly ships

Each week ends with something a design partner can use, so we learn before we build the next layer.

Week 1

Viewer flag + manual "mark stale"

Freshness list and score from human signals only. Five design-partner workspaces.

Week 2

Re-check on next visit

Step-level diff from the extension. Measure precision by hand on 100 flags.

Week 3

Fix routing + propagation

Text update and one-step re-record carried to doc and translations, via Governance.

Week 4

Agent freshness + release trigger

Warnings in Ask Trupeer and MCP; paste release notes to get affected guides.

Quick wins from the reviews, shippable alongside

Credit preview

Show the cost before Generate, Translate or Refresh Voiceover, and say when drafts use credits.

Renewal reminders

Receipt on every charge and an email 7 days before renewal. Both 1-star Trustpilot reviews start with surprises.

Trash with 30-day undo

The FAQ says deleted videos can't be recovered. A safety net makes bold edits safe.

10 · Risks and open questions

What could go wrong

False flags

A/B tests, personalised UI and feature flags change pages without a real change. Mitigation: ship with human signals first, require two signals before an auto-flag, learn from dismissals.

Privacy and access

Re-checks only run on pages the creator opens with the extension. No stored page content beyond what recording keeps today. Smart Blur rules apply.

Credit cannibalisation

Cheaper fixes mean fewer re-records. Open question: price Radar as an Enterprise add-on, or count on the retention and seat expansion that a trusted library brings?

Ownership

Flags without an owner pile up. Open question: default owner = creator, or let admins assign by folder?

12 · 30 · 60 · 90 days

What I'll do after joining

Listen first, ship every week, and tie each release to one number. Radar is my hypothesis; the first 30 days decide whether it's the right bet.

30Days 1 to 30 · Learn

Understand customers, product and numbers

  • Talk to 20+ customers across CS, enablement and PMM, plus sit in on sales and support calls.
  • Read every review, churn note and support ticket; tag them into one shared problem map.
  • Set up Mixpanel or Amplitude funnels: record → generate → edit → share → view, and credit use per account.
  • Learn the codebase basics and the team's Linear flow; join the weekly release.
  • Ship 2 quick wins from the reviews: credit cost preview before Generate, and renewal reminder emails.

Outcome: a baseline dashboard, a ranked problem map the founders agree with, and 2 shipped fixes.

60Days 31 to 60 · Build

Validate and ship the first version

  • Prototype Radar in Figma and with AI tools; test with 5 design-partner workspaces.
  • Ship v1 in weekly slices: viewer flags, freshness list and score, then re-check on next visit.
  • Own one activation metric end to end (e.g. first shared guide within 7 days) and run 2 experiments on it.
  • Set a simple rhythm: one-page PRDs, weekly release notes, a Friday metrics review.

Outcome: Radar v1 live with design partners, flag precision measured, a first activation lift.

90Days 61 to 90 · Scale

Prove impact and plan the next quarter

  • Ship fix routing, one-approval propagation to docs and translations, and agent freshness for Ask Trupeer and MCP.
  • Roll out to all paid workspaces; package the extras for Enterprise with sales.
  • Report results: days from change to fix, fixes without a full re-record, credits per fix.
  • Propose next quarter's roadmap from the problem map, with a clear "not now" list.

Outcome: a feature customers use weekly, numbers to show for it, and a roadmap the team owns.
